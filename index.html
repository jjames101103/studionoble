The actual problem

Bad copy is rarely badly written. It's usually answering a question nobody asked.

A visitor arrives already half-convinced. They like the product, or they wouldn't have clicked. What stops them is something smaller and much more specific — a price that isn't shown, a claim with no proof behind it, a promise that every competitor also makes.

So the page keeps explaining what the business believes in, while the reader is quietly asking "will this leak in my kid's backpack?" or "what does this actually cost?" — and then leaving to find out somewhere else.

That gap is findable. It's usually one paragraph, sometimes one missing sentence. Finding it is the job. Rewriting it is the easy part.

Sample work

Toggle each one. The "before" lines are the kind of copy that's on most small-business sites right now — competent, warm, and doing almost no work.

A bike shop with a café
About section

We are a full-service bicycle shop and café serving the local community. Our experienced staff is committed to providing quality service and a great selection of bikes and accessories. Stop in today and see the difference!

Every good climb has a café partway up.

Ride the mountain passes in Italy and you'll eventually come around a bend to a small café — somewhere to stop, take an espresso standing at the bar, and get back on the bike.

There wasn't one of those on our road. So we built one.

Why it works: the first version could be any bike shop in the country. The second gives the place a reason to exist — and a reason to ride out to it specifically.
A stainless steel lunch box, $84
Product page

Free from plastic, full of purpose. Our beautifully designed lunch boxes are better for your kids and better for the planet — made for real, messy, joyful family moments.

Your fourth lunch box. Hopefully your last.

Compartments so the thing that touched the thing isn't a reason lunch comes home uneaten. Stainless, because it doesn't stain from tomato sauce or hold onto last week's tuna.

Built for the parent who has already tried the other ones.

Why it works: nobody paying $84 is undecided about plastic. They're undecided about you — because the last three lunch boxes failed. Speak to that and the price stops being the obstacle.
A dog collar brand with clip-on charms
Homepage headline

Super cute stuff for your pups! Shop our collection of collars, harnesses and leashes today.

Her collar should change as often as she does.

One collar. A hundred moods. Charms clip on and off in seconds, so the same collar goes spooky in October, sunny in July, and birthday-ready whenever you decide it is.

Why it works: the business isn't collars — it's a collection people keep adding to. The first version sells one purchase. The second sells the next twelve.
